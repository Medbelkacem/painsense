# Synthetic multimodal pain dataset

`dataset.csv` holds 5,760 observation windows drawn from 36 virtual patients, 160 windows
each. A window is one set of simultaneous readings from the three streams; the generator
fixes no duration for it, and the live server in `backend/server.py` emits one per second.
Every value in the file is generated by `ai/simulate.py`. No human subject, no recording
and no clinical record is involved, and nothing here should be read as evidence about real
patients.

Regenerate it with `python ai/simulate.py`. The generator is seeded (20261001), so the
file is reproducible byte for byte.

## Why it exists

The platform fuses three sensor streams and has to decide, window by window, how much to
trust each one. Testing that decision needs data where the streams disagree for known
reasons: a patient whose face is covered, a patch that has come unstuck, an anxious
patient whose heart rate looks exactly like pain. Recorded data with that structure and
with a trustworthy pain label is not something we had, so the windows are simulated and
the simulation is the thing being documented here.

## One row

A row is one window for one patient: 5 identity columns, the label and its companions,
30 model features, 3 signal-quality indices, 4 camera gate flags and 4 therapy state
flags, 49 columns in total.

| group | columns | what they carry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| identity | `patient_id`, `window_index`, `scenario`, `failed_stream`, `clinical_context` | pseudonymous subject, position in the session, what was staged |
| label | `pain_label` | ground truth 0-10, the target |
| reference | `self_report_nrs` | what the patient would say, never a feature |
| reference | `confounder_class` | pain / rest / exercise / anxiety, target of the confounder classifier |
| stream A | 6 columns prefixed `a_` | alpha, beta and theta band power, theta/beta ratio, EMG amplitude in µV and its rise over the patient's own resting tone |
| stream B | 13 columns prefixed `b_` | heart rate, RMSSD and SDNN, tonic GSR in µS and phasic peak count, skin temperature, respiration rate, IMU motion index, Pain Stress Index, and the rise of each over baseline |
| stream C | 11 columns prefixed `c_` | AU4, AU6, AU7, AU9, AU10 and AU43 on the 0-5 FACS intensity scale, contactless rPPG heart rate, voice pitch, jitter, intensity and a voiced flag |
| quality | `a_quality`, `b_quality`, `c_quality` | 0-1 per stream, the input to layer 4 |
| camera gates | `c_smile_detected`, `c_talk_detected`, `c_lighting`, `c_occluded` | conditions that make the face unreadable |
| therapy | `therapy_tens`, `therapy_vibration`, `therapy_thermotherapy`, `therapy_photobiomodulation` | patch output state flags, recorded but not used as features |

## The label and the self-report are different things

`pain_label` is the simulated truth and the only training target. `self_report_nrs` is a
separate column: the number the patient would give on a numeric rating scale, generated
as the label plus a per-patient reporting bias and noise. It is held out of
`STREAM_FEATURES` entirely, so no model in this repository can read it, and `ai/train.py`
asserts that before it trains. It is there so the estimate can be shown next to the
patient's own answer and scored against the same truth. 432 sedated windows have no
self-report at all, because an unconscious patient cannot give one.

## What was staged

| scenario | windows | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pain | 2,515 | pain at an intensity drawn from 1-10 |
| rest | 1,230 | comfortable, label 0 |
| anxiety | 796 | autonomic arousal without nociception, label 0 |
| exercise | 787 | physical exertion, label 0 |
| sedated | 432 | unconscious, pain 3-10, no self-report |

2,813 windows carry label 0 and 2,947 carry a pain level. A further 576 windows, spread
across those scenarios, also carry a sensor failure, written in `failed_stream` as `A`,
`B`, `C` or a combination joined with `+`:

| failure | windows |
| --- | --- |
| one stream | 321 |
| two streams | 179 |
| all three | 76 |

The 76 all-three windows are the ones that should make the fusion refuse to answer.

## The confounders

This is the part that matters. Exercise and anxiety were built to be indistinguishable
from pain on the autonomic stream alone, which is what makes the weighting problem real.

| group | heart rate | respiration | GSR µS | skin temp Δ | motion | EMG Δ µV | pain AUs | PSI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rest | 70.0 | 14.4 | 4.85 | +0.01 | 0.17 | −0.05 | 0.43 | 7.4 |
| pain 7-10 | 85.6 | 17.9 | 7.16 | −0.38 | 0.26 | 8.55 | 2.57 | 36.2 |
| exercise | 99.4 | 21.9 | 6.96 | +0.87 | 0.66 | −0.05 | 0.44 | 52.9 |
| anxiety | 84.1 | 17.4 | 7.75 | −0.37 | 0.28 | 0.09 | 0.43 | 35.9 |

Read the anxiety row against the pain row. Heart rate, respiration, skin conductance,
skin temperature and the Pain Stress Index are all but identical. Only the EMG rise
(0.09 against 8.55) and the pain action units (0.43 against 2.57) separate them, and both
of those live on streams A and C. Exercise raises the Pain Stress Index higher than real
pain does, 52.9 against 36.2, and leaves EMG and the pain action units flat.

Two deliberate choices keep this honest. Anxiety cools the skin as pain does, so skin
temperature cannot be used as a shortcut to tell them apart. Exercise motion is drawn
from 0.34 to 0.97 while pain motion reaches past 0.34 often enough to overlap, so the
IMU narrows the question without settling it.

## Signal quality means something

A quality index is not decoration here. Each stream's noise is scaled by its own quality
in the same window, so a low index really is a corrupted reading:

- stream A: electrode contact is drawn per window and slips below 0.35 in about 6% of
  windows. Band power and EMG noise are multiplied by `1 + 6(1 − contact)²`. The EMG rise
  correlates 0.65 with the label when contact is good and 0.15 when it is poor.
- stream B: motion and patch adhesion scale the noise on every autonomic channel by
  `1 + 2.5·motion + 5(1 − adhesion)`. Adhesion fails in about 5% of windows.
- stream C: lighting, occlusion and motion combine into a clarity term, and action-unit
  noise is `0.45(1 + 4(1 − clarity))`. An occluded face produces action units that are
  mostly noise.

1,226 windows (21.3%) have an occluded face, 525 a smile and 789 speech. Median quality
is 0.891 for A, 0.864 for B and 0.667 for C.

## Patients differ, and that is where the difficulty lives

Each virtual patient gets its own resting physiology and its own response gains: how
strongly the autonomic system reacts, how expressive the face is (0.20 to 1.60), how much
the muscles guard, how noisy the electrodes are, how labile the baseline is, and how
honestly the self-report tracks the truth.

Face access is a patient trait rather than a per-window accident, because in a ward it is:
an oxygen mask, a prone position or a dressing covers the face for a whole stay, not for a
second. Four of the 36 patients have their face obstructed in more than half their
windows. Since `ai/train.py` splits by patient, a face-only estimator meets those patients
for the first time in the test set, which is the point.

## Split

`ai/train.py` holds out 11 patients and trains on the other 25, so no patient appears on
both sides. The held-out set is VP-01, VP-05, VP-10, VP-11, VP-13, VP-19, VP-20, VP-23,
VP-25, VP-29 and VP-30: 1,760 windows against 4,000.

On the training split, out-of-fold stream scores correlate with the label at 0.794 for A,
0.611 for B and 0.838 for C. Those three numbers are what layer 2 turns into weights.

## Limits

The generator is a set of algebraic response curves with Gaussian noise, not a physiological
model. Band powers are not generated from a simulated EEG, the action units are numbers
rather than rendered faces, and voice features are drawn directly rather than synthesised
from audio. Windows within a patient are independent draws, so there is no temporal
autocorrelation, no drift over a session and no carry-over between windows; a model that
exploited time would learn nothing here. The pain label is a free parameter rather than a
consequence of an injury or a drug.

What the data does support is the question this prototype asks: given three streams of
differing and changing reliability, how much should each one count, and when should the
system decline to answer. It supports no claim about accuracy on real patients.
